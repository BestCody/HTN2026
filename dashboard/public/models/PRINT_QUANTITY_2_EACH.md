# Print quantity

Print **two copies of every STL in this folder** for two complete arms.

Do not print the mock-servo STL as a structural robot component. It is stored separately in `MOCK_SERVOS`.

Recommended starting profile: 0.20 mm layers, 0.4 mm nozzle, five walls, six top/bottom layers, and 30-35% gyroid or cubic infill. Use local solid infill at gear roots, horn pockets, journal roots, and bolted lugs.

For the exact PLA profile for every part, use `PLA_BLACK_PRINT_SETTINGS.csv` and `PLA_BLACK_PRINT_SETTINGS.md` in this folder.
